# Fnality International

> Fnality International is a London-headquartered financial market infrastructure company that builds and operates distributed-ledger wholesale payment systems settling in tokenised central bank money. Backed by 24 global financial institutions, Fnality launched the Sterling Fnality Payment System (£FnPS) in 2024 — the first regulated DLT-based payment system supervised by the Bank of England — with US Dollar and Euro payment systems in development. The systems run on private Ethereum networks and give participant banks 24/7 availability, real-time atomic and PvP settlement, programmable money, and T+0 cross-border liquidity movement.

**Fnality publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL specification, no SDK in any package registry, no MCP server and no agent card. Technical documentation exists but sits in a credential-gated Archbee space at https://docs.fnality.com/, which returns a magic-link login rather than content. Integration is arranged bilaterally with admitted participant institutions through Fnality Services. This file is generated by API Evangelist from public pages only; it is not published by Fnality.

## Payment systems

- [Sterling Fnality Payment System (£FnPS)](https://fnality.com/payment-systems/british-sterling-payment-system): The first fully regulated DLT-based payment system, live since 2024 and supervised by the Bank of England.
- [US Dollar Payment System](https://fnality.com/payment-systems/us-dollar-payment-system): USD Fnality Payment System, in development.
- [Euro Payment System](https://fnality.com/payment-systems/euro-payment-system): EUR Fnality Payment System, in development.

## How it works

- [How it works](https://fnality.com/how-it-works): Private Ethereum networks, central-bank-backed funds, consensus-based finality, no centralised settlement agent.
- [Fnality Services](https://fnality.com/how-it-works/fnality-services): Operational support and product delivery arm — daily operations, software licensing to participants, inter-system arrangements, risk management.
- [Benefits](https://fnality.com/benefits): 24/7 availability, real-time settlement, programmability of money, liquidity optimisation.
- [Use cases](https://fnality.com/use-cases): Cross-border payments, intraday liquidity, collateral mobility, digital securities settlement.
